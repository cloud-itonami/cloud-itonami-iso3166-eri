# Operator guide — ERI

Portal: no e-procurement portal, dedicated public-tender board or
general government-procurement law could be found for Eritrea in this
iteration -- notices, when they exist, are not published on any
independently-reachable government surface this iteration could locate.
Entity: MTI trade license permit (Ministry of Trade and Industry) +
Inland Revenue Department tax record (Ministry of Finance). Mining/
mineral-exploration engagements additionally clear the Mining Law's own
domestic-supplier-preference / minimum-three-supplier-RFO compliance
gate before a filing can proceed (Minerals Proclamation No. 68/1995 +
Mineral Proclamation No. 165/2011 + Legal Notice No. 19/1995 + Mining
Income Tax Proclamation No. 69/1995). Human-gated filing only.
